Discovery Two years of silence The building as answer Twelve guests Material honesty

VEIL was not designed. It was discovered. The eastern face of this limestone ridge had been shaping light since before the valley below had a name. We found it on foot, in the dry season, following a cartographer's mistake that led us three hours off the route we had planned.

We sat with it for two years before we drew a single line. The question was never: what should this be? The question was: what does this already know?

The building answers the ridge. The ridge answers the light. The light answers the hour. The hour answers the guest. We are simply the translation layer between stone and skin.

Twelve guests at a time. Not because we cannot accommodate more. Because more would change what this is.

Every decision here was made in the presence of the place. The position of each opening was tested at dawn and at last light before it was fixed. The thermal pool was placed where a natural spring already surfaced. The linen was undyed because the walls already held their own colour. We do not decorate. We pay attention.
